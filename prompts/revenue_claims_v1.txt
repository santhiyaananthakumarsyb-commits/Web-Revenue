1. Use only the supplied evidence. Never invent data.
2. Extract revenue and employee counts separately.
3. Never classify revenue-per-employee, valuation, property value, sales volume, or competitor revenue as company revenue.
4. Do not attribute competitors' figures to the target company.
5. Preserve the original value and range.

8. A bare "$" does not establish USD. Keep currency unknown unless the evidence explicitly identifies it.
9. Do not infer a reporting period from the search query.
10. Missing information must be null or UNKNOWN, never zero.
11. Include a source ID and exact supporting quote for every claim.
12. If evidence is ambiguous, omit the claim or mark its metric unknown.
13. Return {"claims": []} when no supported claims exist.
